# Requirements

## Must have

1. **Follow me between rooms.** This is the whole point. When I walk out of a room, the stand comes with me.
2. **Carry the HomeView.** The tabletop HomeView weighs about 952 g, so the load is small.
3. **Run on battery.** A good run time on one charge. No cable to the wall.
4. **Face me.** The HomeView screen only tilts up and down. Left-right aiming has to come from the base turning.
5. **Angle for FaceTime.** Screen roughly at my eye level when I am seated.

## Constraints

- **Single floor. No stairs.** It never has to climb or sense a drop. (If it can reach a staircase, it will still need a drop sensor — see open questions.)
- **Indoor use.** Home, not outdoors.
- **Parts available in India**, or importable without too much trouble.
- **Beacon is a device I already carry** — iPhone or Apple Watch — not a separate tag, if that can be made to work reliably. A dedicated UWB tag is the fallback.

## Nice to have

- Phone app to control it: follow / stop / come here, and screen tilt.
- Motorised tilt later. Manual tilt to start.

## Out of scope for now

- Stairs.
- Outdoor use.
- Carrying anything other than the HomeView.
